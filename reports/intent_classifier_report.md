## What this number is, and is not

This is a held-out slice of the corpus the model trained on. It measures
that the model learned the corpus — not that the corpus resembles what a
user types. For the second question see
[`real_command_evidence.md`](real_command_evidence.md), which evaluates
the app's own advertised commands and paraphrases of them.

## Which model production actually loads

`get_trained_classifier()` fits **Logistic Regression**, not necessarily the
winner above. On this corpus Logistic Regression fits in 1.58s against 66.89s for SVM, for +0.0093 macro-F1 — and the model is refitted whenever the cached artefact is missing, including on a fresh clone. The comparison is the coursework deliverable; the production choice is a latency trade-off, and stating both is the point.
